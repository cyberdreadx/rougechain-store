About this game

Gltch Runner blends cyberpunk city life with first- and third-person combat. Someone took eleven days out of your head and filed them under another name. Make a home in Kestrel Block, take work from its locals, and follow the story beyond your doorstep.

A block that lives

Meet the locals around Kestrel Block, pick up paid work, stock your kitchen and return to flat 306. In Early Access, ride Line 6 to Neon Row and visit AFTERIMAGE.

Morning smog turns to a pink sunset and then a wet neon night. When it rains, the street fills with reflections.

Day · sunset · night · rain

Eleven days

Dead Signal takes you down into NEXUS's Sublevel 3. After it come the eleven days themselves. Vale bottled them, and a bottled day won't take without its anchor: something real from the place it happened.

Recover an anchor, bring it home, and remember. The opening introduces your place in the block before the story leads down into NEXUS and out along the Cold Line.

Day Two · The Cold Line · Halt 14

Between jobs

Flat 306 in Kestrel Heights has your bed, your weapons locker and a balcony over the plaza. Eat and drink before you rest, fit gear at the workbench, and keep everything you carry in a bag that shows what it does and what it's worth.

Earn and spend XRGE, the block's coin, and stake it with KESTREL-1 for a little back each day. It's in-game money only.

Inventory · workbench · food and rest · 10 Genesis Papers to find

Move like a runner

Sprint, slide, double jump, mantle and run along walls. Tap to hack, put a drone up, throw a grenade, or holster the gun and draw the MonoEdge.

Switch between first person and an over-the-shoulder view in story or multiplayer. Use walls and crates for cover, then peek out to aim.
